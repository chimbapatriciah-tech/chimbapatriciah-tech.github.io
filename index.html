The problems we take off your desk

When the admin piles up,
your important work gets delayed.

PC Green Solutions provides practical hands-on support for the recurring tasks that can distract a small or international team from serving customers and growing its business.

01

Scattered documents and records

Files are difficult to find, information is spread across folders and routine records need updating. We organise agreed documents, trackers and digital records.

02

Emails and follow-ups left hanging

Routine messages, reminders and supplier responses take time. We prepare agreed correspondence, track actions and follow up on outstanding items.

03

Meetings without clear next steps

Scheduling, agendas, notes and action lists can slip. We coordinate meetings and keep agreed actions and deadlines visible.

04

Too many moving parts

Small projects involve several people, dates and details. We maintain simple schedules and coordinate practical updates so everyone knows what happens next.

05

Language and coordination friction

International teams may need clearer routine communication. We offer English-first support and German-language assistance for agreed everyday correspondence.

06

Not enough time for core business

Owners and teams spend valuable hours on repeatable admin. We take on defined support tasks so you can focus on customers, decisions and delivery.

We provide administrative and coordination support, not legal, tax, accounting, payroll or regulated professional advice. Any communication or external action is agreed with you in advance.
